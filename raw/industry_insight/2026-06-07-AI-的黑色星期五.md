# AI's Black Friday

**Published:** June 6, 2026

## Overview

Gary Marcus analyzes a significant market downturn affecting AI-related companies, with approximately half a trillion dollars in market value disappearing overnight. Major semiconductor and AI companies experienced substantial losses exceeding the broader market decline.

## Key Market Impacts

The Dow fell 1.35%, but AI-focused sectors fared worse:
- Chip manufacturers (NVIDIA, Broadcom, Micron) saw larger drops
- GPU lessors (CoreWeave, Nebius) experienced significant declines
- Major tech companies (Oracle, Microsoft, Meta) also declined sharply
- South Korea's KOSPI dropped 5.5%, with Samsung down 6.4% and SK Hynix falling 9.9%

## Critical Developments

**Government Involvement:** Reports emerged of discussions between the Trump administration and OpenAI regarding "a possible government stake in the AI startup." Marcus characterizes this as a bailout disguised as investment, noting that "no path to profitability" exists.

**Geopolitical Concerns:** A government stake could undermine trust in American AI companies globally, potentially triggering a new "cold war" over AI development.

**GPU Oversupply:** Elon Musk's SpaceX announced leasing 110,000 GPUs to Google and 220,000 to Anthropic, suggesting massive overcapacity rather than the chip scarcity predicted previously.

## Broader Implications

Marcus emphasizes that infrastructure vendors face headwinds when major companies resort to equity sales to fund continued AI investments, describing the situation as "a black hole" consuming capital without demonstrable returns.
